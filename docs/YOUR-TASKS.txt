YEZDI CONNECT - YOUR 2 TASKS
============================

These two recordings tell us how your bike talks over Bluetooth. Do them when you
are free, then share the files in the chat. Task 1 takes about 15 minutes, Task 2
about 30 minutes (including a short ride).

You need: your Yezdi Adventure, your Galaxy M31, and the official Jawa/Yezdi app.


------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 1 - Scan the bike with nRF Connect  (what the bike exposes)
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Install "nRF Connect for Mobile" (by Nordic Semiconductor) from the Play Store.

2. Close the official Yezdi app completely (swipe it away from recent apps) and turn
   off its auto-connect if it has one. The bike usually allows only ONE Bluetooth
   connection at a time.

3. Turn the bike's ignition ON.

4. Open nRF Connect -> SCANNER tab -> tap SCAN.
   Find the bike. Its name may contain Yezdi, Jawa or CL. If unsure, walk closer to
   the bike: the right device's signal (dBm number) gets stronger.
   Tap CONNECT.

5. You'll see a list of "services". Tap each one to expand it.
   Take SCREENSHOTS of everything: every service and every characteristic, with
   their properties (READ / WRITE / NOTIFY / INDICATE).

6. For every characteristic that has NOTIFY or INDICATE, tap the icon with the
   three down-arrows to turn notifications on.

7. Now do these one at a time, and WRITE DOWN THE TIME (with seconds if you can)
   of each one:
     a) ignition off, then on again
     b) start the engine
     c) rev the engine a few times
     d) ride a short distance (a minute or two)
     e) switch/reset the trip meter
     f) anything else the dash shows (low fuel warning, indicators, etc.)

8. For every characteristic that has READ, tap the single down-arrow icon once.

9. Open the LOG tab (swipe from the left / menu -> Log, depending on version)
   -> share/export the log as a file.

SEND ME:
  - the exported log file
  - all screenshots
  - your list of actions with their times


------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 2 - Record a Bluetooth log while the official app navigates
          (what the official app sends to the dash)
------------------------------------------------------------

Part A - Turn on Bluetooth logging  (Galaxy M31 / One UI steps)
1. Settings -> About phone -> Software information -> tap "Build number"
   7 times. Enter your PIN if asked. "Developer options" now appears at the
   very bottom of Settings.
2. Settings -> Developer options -> turn on
   "Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log".
3. Turn Bluetooth OFF and then ON again (this starts the log).

Part B - Use the official app, and WRITE DOWN THE TIME of each step
4. Open the official Yezdi app and connect to the bike.
5. Start navigation in the official app to somewhere close, on a route with
   several left and right turns and, if possible, a roundabout.
   Ride it (or at least the first few turns).
6. Get a test phone call from a known number (ask a friend, or call yourself
   from another phone). Let it show on the dash.
7. Get a test SMS. Let it show on the dash.
8. Open the app's other screens once (stats, settings, etc.).

   NOTE: the log will contain the test caller's number/name and the SMS text,
   so use a test message you don't mind sharing.

Part C - Get the log file  (Galaxy M31 / Samsung method)
9. Do NOT turn the snoop log off yet. Samsung copies it out with its
   service menu:
10. Open the Phone app and dial:  *#9900#
    (the "SysDump" screen opens; nothing is called).
11. Tap "Run dumpstate/logcat" and wait until it finishes (a minute or two).
12. Tap "Copy to sdcard (include CP Ramdump)".
    Then tap "Exit" (or press back). Do NOT tap anything else in that menu.
13. Open the "My Files" app -> Internal storage -> "log" folder.
    Look for a file with "btsnoop" in its name (it may be inside a "bt"
    sub-folder or have a date in its name), e.g. btsnoop_hci.log.
14. Now go back to Developer options and turn
    "Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log" OFF.

If there is no btsnoop file in the log folder, use the general method:
    Developer options -> "Take bug report" -> Full report -> wait for the
    notification -> save the zip -> inside it search for "btsnoop"
    (often FS/data/log/bt/ or FS/data/misc/bluetooth/logs/ on Samsung).

SEND ME:
  - ONLY the btsnoop_hci.log file (NOT the whole bug report zip, because it
    has a lot of personal data)
  - your list of steps with their times


------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Times matter a lot: they let me match each Bluetooth message to what you did.
- If a step doesn't work on your phone, skip it and tell me what you saw.
- If you get stuck, send me a screenshot and I'll help.
